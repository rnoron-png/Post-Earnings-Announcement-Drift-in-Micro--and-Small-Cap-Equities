# Post-Earnings-Announcement-Drift-in-Micro--and-Small-Cap-Equities
A long-only post-earnings-announcement drift (PEAD) trading strategy for the micro- and small-cap market segment built on QuantConnect's LEAN engine

Post-earnings-announcement-drift is one of the most widely studied and enduring anomalies in empirical finance. In liquid, heavily arbitraged markets the effect has been competed away, weakening the strategy’s returns. In the least liquid parts of the market, like micro- and small-cap equities, institutional capital is constrained leading to different market conditions that are comparatively less explored. This paper studies a long-only PEAD strategy in US micro- and small-capitalization equities from the period 2015 to 2024, using a price-reaction surprise proxy and using the QuantConnect default fee model for US equities. The strategy produces economically meaningful returns of 8.9% and 12.2% compounded annually and positive risk-adjusted alpha net of fees across versions. The return attribution analysis isolates the earnings surprise signal from broader market exposure. The strategy further sweeps idle capital into treasury bills, which increases risk-adjusted returns with no effect on drawdown. The illiquid nature of the strategy leads to capacity limits, which are estimated and analyzed to link to the constraints that sustain the anomaly. This study finds a friction-sensitive strategy that generates economically meaningful returns while reserving the 2025+ period for out-of-sample future verification.
